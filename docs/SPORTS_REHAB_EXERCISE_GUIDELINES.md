This clinical guide provides exhaustive rehabilitation protocols for the top athletic musculoskeletal conditions managed through the AthleteCare Medical Clinic portal.

### Protocol 1: Acute Hamstring Complex Rehabilitation (Biceps Femoris Long Head)

#### Pathomechanics & Biomechanical Overview
Hamstring injuries represent up to 24% of all collegiate sports injuries, most commonly occurring during the late swing phase of maximum velocity sprinting when the muscle complex undergoes rapid eccentric elongation.

#### Phase 1: Acute Protection & Cellular Homeostasis (Days 1–5)
- **Primary Clinical Goals:** Control interstitial hematoma, minimize intramuscular edema, re-establish voluntary motor unit activation.
- **Contraindications:** Avoid static over-stretching into sharp pain ($>3/10$ visual analog scale).
- **Prescribed Exercise Regimen:**
  1. *Submaximal Prone Isometric Knee Flexion:* 5 angles ($30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ, 75^\circ, 90^\circ$), 5-second holds, 4 repetitions per angle.
  2. *Supine Pelvic Bridges (Bilateral):* 3 sets $\times$ 12 repetitions with 2-second isometric squeeze at terminal extension.
  3. *Active Slump Neurodynamic Flossing:* 3 sets $\times$ 10 gentle repetitions to preserve sciatic nerve excursion.
  4. *Low-Impact Monitored Ergometry:* 15 minutes at $60\text{ RPM}$ below $110\text{ BPM}$ heart rate.

#### Phase 2: Neuromuscular Control & Lengthening Under Load (Days 6–14)
- **Primary Clinical Goals:** Promote collagen fiber alignment along lines of tensile stress, restore hamstring-to-quadriceps strength ratio ($H:Q \ge 0.60$).
- **Prescribed Exercise Regimen:**
  1. *Nordic Hamstring Curl (Assisted Band Eccentric):* 4 sets $\times$ 6 repetitions with 4-second controlled descent.
  2. *Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift (RDL):* Dumbbell load 15% body weight, 3 sets $\times$ 8 repetitions per limb.
  3. *Prone Resistance Band Knee Flexion:* 4 sets $\times$ 12 repetitions with 3-second eccentric return.
  4. *High-Knee "A-Skips" & Ankling Drills:* Track surface, 4 sets $\times$ 20 meters.

#### Phase 3: High-Velocity Deceleration & Sport-Specific Re-Integration (Days 15+)
- **Primary Clinical Goals:** Recondition tolerance to maximal eccentric peak force, simulate match deceleration demands.
- **Prescribed Regimen:**
  1. *Unassisted Nordic Hamstring Curls:* 3 sets $\times$ 8 repetitions.
  2. *Sprint Deceleration Stop-and-Cuts:* $10\text{m}$ acceleration to $90\%$ velocity, abrupt deceleration within $2\text{m}$ zone $\times 6$ reps.
  3. *Full Competitive Practice Scrimmage:* Monitored with ACWR $<1.20$.

### Protocol 2: Lateral Ankle Sprain (ATFL & CFL Ligamentous Complex)

#### Phase 1: Acute Protection & Edema Evacuation
- **Clinical Modalities:** P.E.A.C.E. and L.O.V.E. protocol. Avoid prolonged complete immobilization.
- **Prescribed Exercises:**
  1. *Alphabet Dorsi/Plantar/Inversion Active Foot Tracing:* 3 complete cycles $\times 3$ daily.
  2. *Isometric Peroneal Wall Press:* 4 sets $\times 15$-second holds.
  3. *Unweighted Seated Calf Pumps:* 3 sets $\times 25$ reps to stimulate calf muscle veno-lymphatic pump.

#### Phase 2: Dynamic Proprioceptive Stability
- **Prescribed Exercises:**
  1. *Single-Leg Stance on Foam Pad / Airex:* 4 sets $\times 45$ seconds eyes open, progressing to eyes closed.
  2. *Star Excursion Balance Test (SEBT) Reach Drills:* Anterior, posteromedial, posterolateral excursions $\times 5$ circuits.
  3. *Multi-Planar Mini-Trampoline Hops:* 3 sets $\times 15$ continuous soft-landing hops.
